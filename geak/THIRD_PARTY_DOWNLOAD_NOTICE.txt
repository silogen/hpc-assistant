HPC Assistant — GEAK Third-Party Download Notice
========================================================

When you enable GEAK or submit a GEAK job, the script
setup-geak.sh automatically downloads and installs additional software from
third-party sources. This runs on a GPU compute node inside your SLURM
job.

Components downloaded or installed include (non-exhaustive):

  - GEAK — https://github.com/AMD-AGI/GEAK (cloned from GitHub)
  - Metrix (intellikit) — https://github.com/AMDResearch/intellikit (cloned)
  - fastmcp and other Python packages from PyPI
  - GEAK MCP tooling packages from the GEAK repository

These components are subject to their own copyright and license terms. See
THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt in the HPC Assistant repository for copyright and
license details.

AMD does not vend these third-party components in the HPC Assistant
source repository. They are fetched at job runtime when you invoke setup-geak.sh
(via run-geak.sbatch or submit-geak-job.sh).

By accepting this notice, you acknowledge that:

  1. Your GEAK jobs will download third-party software from external networks
     (for example GitHub and PyPI).
  2. You are responsible for compliance with your site's acceptable-use,
     security, and network policies.
  3. You have had the opportunity to review the applicable third-party
     licenses in THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt.

To decline, do not enable GEAK during setup and do not submit GEAK jobs.
